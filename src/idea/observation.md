Absolutely. For **MIRROR**, I’d design the app less like a normal productivity app and more like a **personal behavioral laboratory**—calm, premium, evidence-driven, with the “aha” moments front and center.

## MIRROR — Complete App Screen Architecture

### Primary navigation

**Bottom navigation:**

1. **Mirror** — insights + current behavioral picture
2. **Timeline** — what actually happened
3. **Experiments** — test what changes outcomes
4. **You** — personal model, goals, data & settings

Plus a floating **Reflect** action for quick voice/text reflection.

## The 5 screens I'd make **visually spectacular**

If we're optimizing MIRROR specifically for **Shipaton judging**, I'd put disproportionate design effort into:

**1. Mirror Home**
The user instantly understands what the product does.

**2. Evidence View**
Pattern → raw evidence → confidence.
This makes the AI feel trustworthy.

**3. Personal Model**
Interactive behavioral graph.
Huge Design Award potential.

**4. Experiment Live/Result**
Before → intervention → after.
This communicates the unique product loop in seconds.

**5. “What Don't I Know About Myself?”**
The signature experience.

```text
What don't I know
about myself?

MIRROR found 3 patterns
you haven't explicitly noticed.

        ↓

01
You tend to abandon projects
after their scope changes.

Confidence 86%

        ↓

02
Your best work follows
a predictable preparation ritual.

Confidence 81%

        ↓

03
You underestimate how much
context switching costs you.

Confidence 74%

       [ Explore ]
```

That last screen could literally become the **MIRROR demo climax**:

**Observe → Discover → Explain → Experiment → Measure → Learn.**

That's the product—not 30 disconnected screens. The entire UI should constantly reinforce that one loop.
